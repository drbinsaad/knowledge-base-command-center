## Supplemental Mac Obsidian evidence

The built candidate was installed into a disposable test vault and exercised inside real Obsidian 1.13.7 on macOS with `app.emulateMobile(true)`. This was Obsidian's Electron desktop runtime, not an iPhone or iOS WebKit.

- At a 400 × 1267 portrait viewport, the Command Center, Organizer Notes step, selected-note tray, Destinations step, two cross-base destination cards, exact Review, active-note organization indicator, and read-only membership dialog were inspected successfully.
- At a 1267 × 400 landscape viewport, the first run exposed a footer layout defect that consumed too much Review space. A targeted landscape/coarse-pointer fix was applied, automated coverage was added, and the same real Obsidian viewport was retested with the before/after Review content and footer actions reachable.
- Apply was not activated during this supplemental inspection. No Markdown file was created, moved, renamed, deleted, or rewritten, and no KBCC organization was intentionally changed.
- Automated real-Chromium coverage also exercised the Organizer at LTR 1267 × 400 and RTL 844 × 390 coarse-pointer landscape viewports, alongside the remaining layout variants.

These observations support the desktop-emulated responsive layout only. They do not establish physical touch behavior, iOS WebKit behavior, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, Arabic iOS direction, software-keyboard or safe-area behavior, Sync handoff, interruption recovery, or a physical-device Pass.
