# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Knowledge Base Command Center welcomes respectful participation from people of every background and experience level.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- giving specific, constructive, and technically grounded feedback;
- respecting different experience levels, devices, languages, and workflows;
- protecting other people's privacy and removing sensitive vault data from examples;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing, and correcting course when harm occurs; and
- keeping discussion focused on the project and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, or personal attacks;
- trolling, sustained disruption, or publishing another person's private information;
- pressuring someone to disclose private vault, medical, employment, or identity information;
- using project channels for spam, unrelated promotion, or professional/medical solicitation; and
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source community.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, Discussions if enabled, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants according to the seriousness and pattern of behavior. Enforcement decisions should explain the affected behavior without unnecessarily exposing a reporter or private information.

Do not place sensitive conduct reports or personal details in a public issue.

- Use GitHub's **Report abuse or spam** tools for urgent platform abuse.
- If the repository Security page currently offers **Report a vulnerability**, it can provide a private repository channel; clearly label the report as a conduct concern rather than a vulnerability.
- If no private project channel is available, use any private contact method the maintainer has published on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/drbinsaad). This repository does not promise that such a method will always be present.

The maintainer will handle reports in good faith, avoid conflicts of interest where practical, and preserve confidentiality to the extent the available channel allows.
